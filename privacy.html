About Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kinetic Melody ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our mobile applications, including Target Tuner, StageCut, ChordSeek, ChordSeek: Mandolin, ScaleSeek: Guitar Scales, ScaleSeek: Mandolin Scales, and ScaleSeek: Bass Scales (the "Apps"). By using an App, you agree to the practices described below.

Media Access (Photos, Videos & Audio Files)

StageCut requires access to the video, photo, and audio files stored on your device so that you can select and edit them within the App. Editing is performed entirely on your device. We do not access, upload, or transmit your media files to us or to any third party.

Microphone Access

StageCut may request microphone access to support certain recording-related features. If this permission is requested, audio is processed on your device and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by us or shared with any third party.

Chord Engine & Playback (ChordSeek)

ChordSeek's chord finder, chord charts, and audio playback all run entirely on your device in both ChordSeek and ChordSeek: Mandolin. Neither App accesses your microphone, photos, or files, and neither requires an account or sign-up to use.

Scale Engine & Playback (ScaleSeek)

ScaleSeek's scale finder, fretboard diagrams, and audio playback all run entirely on your device in the Guitar Scales, Mandolin Scales, and Bass Scales Apps. None of these Apps accesses your microphone, photos, or files, and none requires an account or sign-up to use.

Our Website (kineticmelody.org)

The free online tools on this website, including the chord finders, Chords for a Key and the Circle of Fifths, run entirely in your browser; the chords you search for and the keys you pick are not sent to us. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It records standard visit data such as the pages viewed, the approximate location (country and city), the device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site, and it sets cookies to tell visits apart. This data is not linked to your name or contact information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics cookies are turned off by default. You can also block Google Analytics in any browser with Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our Apps use the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads within the free version of the App and may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and other data used for ad delivery and personalization. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — used by ChordSeek, ChordSeek: Mandolin, StageCut, and ScaleSeek: Guitar Scales to measure whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs, and to show those ads to people more likely to find the App useful. When one of these Apps is installed and opened, it sends Meta the install and app launch events, together with your device's advertising identifier and basic device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, and language. In regions where we ask for your consent, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, this happens only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy Options at the bottom of that App's home screen. ScaleSeek: Mandolin Scales and ScaleSeek: Bass Scales do not use the Meta SDK. You can review Meta's practices at facebook.com/privacy/policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card details; these are handled directly by Google Play.
  • Google Play Console — provides us with aggregated, anonymized crash reports and install statistics to help us maintain and improve the Apps.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymized usage events such as which screens you open, which chords and playback modes you use, which practice progressions and tempos you start, the outcome of billing attempts, and whether you're on the free or paid tier of the App. None of this is linked to your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — sends us a report if an App crashes, with technical details about the error and your device, such as the device model and operating system version, so we can find and fix the problem. Crash reports are not linked to your name, email address, or contact information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact information directly. We do not sell personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is not configured for child-directed treatment.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because our Apps do not require an account, we hold no data tied to your identity that we could delete on request. Analytics events are anonymized, are not linked to your name or contact information, and are retained according to Firebase Analytics' standard retention settings before being automatically deleted. You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads, at any time from your device's system settings. Information sent to Meta through ChordSeek, ChordSeek: Mandolin, StageCut, or ScaleSeek: Guitar Scales is kept and deleted according to Meta's own policies.

Data Security

Because media editing and any microphone-based features are processed on-device and never leave your phone, there is no server-side storage of your media or audio data to secure. Data handled by third-party services above is protected according to their respective security practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including if we add features such as cloud export or additional network functionality in the future. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@kineticmelody.org, or reach our support team at support@kineticmelody.org.
