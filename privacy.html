Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kinetic Melody ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our mobile applications, including Target Tuner (the "Apps"). By using an App, you agree to the practices described below.

Microphone Access

Target Tuner uses your device's microphone to detect the pitch of the note you are playing. This audio is processed entirely on your device in real time to display tuning feedback. We do not record, store, or transmit your microphone audio, and it is never sent to us or to any third party.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our Apps use the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays ads within the free version of the App and may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and other data used for ad delivery and personalization. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases (such as the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade). We do not receive or store your payment card details; these are handled directly by Google Play.
  • Google Play Console — provides us with aggregated, anonymized crash reports and install statistics to help us maintain and improve the Apps.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact information directly. We do not sell personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is not configured for child-directed treatment.

Data Security

Because tuning audio is processed on-device and never leaves your phone, there is no server-side storage of your microphone data to secure. Data handled by third-party services above is protected according to their respective security practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@kineticmelody.org, or reach our support team at support@kineticmelody.org.
