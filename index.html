ChordSeek Find Your Chords. Faster.

You already know one way to play a G — ChordSeek shows you the rest. Pick a root and a quality and get every playable shape across the fretboard, ranked by the voicings guitarists actually reach for, each drawn as a full chord chart with finger numbers, note names, and intervals.

  • All 12 roots across 9 chord qualities, ranked by real-world playability
  • Open, barre, and movable shapes up the whole neck
  • Tap to hear any voicing — strummed or arpeggiated
  • Triads and inversions mapped across string sets
  • 15 built-in practice progressions with tempo, click, and strum patterns
  • No account needed — free, supported by ads
