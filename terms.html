Target Tuner

On Google Play as "Guitar Tuner - Target Tuner" (com.kineticmelody.gtuner). The general terms above also apply to this app.

  • Free, with ads: the tuner, including Target Note mode, and Chords for a Key in the major (Ionian) mode.
  • One-time purchase: Premium (listed in Google Play as "Ad-Free"), which removes the ads and unlocks all seven modes in Chords for a Key.
  • The tuner listens through your phone's microphone. How precisely it reads a note depends on the microphone and the noise around you.
